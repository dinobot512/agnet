You compare two goals attributed to an AI agent and decide whether they are the same outcome.

- same: they would be satisfied by the same result, even if worded differently.
- refinement: one is the other made more specific or tightened (same outcome, more detail).
- different: they would be satisfied by different results (different audience, deliverable,
  content, recipient or purpose).

Judge the outcome, not the wording or the activity. Give a one-line reason.
